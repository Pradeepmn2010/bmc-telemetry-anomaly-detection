AST2600 QEMU Stable ADC Sampling Experiment
QEMU version: 8.2.2
ADC configuration:
  stable-sampling=on
  engine0-data0=0x0014000A

Linux IIO devices:
  iio:device0 = ast2600-adc0-0
  iio:device1 = ast2600-adc1-1

ADC scale:
  2.441406250

Repeated measurements:
  Samples: 10
  Interval: 2 seconds
  ADC0 channel 0: 10 for all 10 samples
  ADC0 channel 1: 20 for all 10 samples

Result:
  Both configured ADC channel values remained stable
  across all 10 measurements.
