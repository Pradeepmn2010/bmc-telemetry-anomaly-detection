A/B EXPERIMENT COMPARISON
Date: 2026-11-08

Environment:
- Fresh OpenBMC AST2600 image
- QEMU 8.2.2 with modified ADC model
- ADC sensor daemon stopped during recorded measurements
- Manual IIO reads at approximately 2-second intervals

EXPERIMENT A: STABLE SAMPLING ON
CH0: 10, 10, 10, 10, 10
CH1: 20, 20, 20, 20, 20

EXPERIMENT B: STABLE SAMPLING OFF
CH0: 187, 197, 207, 217, 227
CH1: 70, 84, 98, 112, 126

RESULT:
Stable mode produced constant readings.
Control mode produced increasing synthetic readings.

NOTE:
Both modes were configured with the same initial register
values. Control-mode readings had already advanced before
the recorded manual samples, due to earlier register reads.
These are synthetic QEMU measurements, not physical sensor data.
